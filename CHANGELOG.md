# Changelog

## 1.0.0
First release.
- Floating command bar with fuzzy search, categories, favorites and recents
- Video effects, audio effects, video transitions
- Keyframe-safe captured presets with timing modes and per-property toggles
- Macros with pauses and per-step transition options
- Custom item shortcuts and rebindable panel keys
- Five themes, accent color and layout options
- Cue Bridge UXP plugin with auto-reconnect and pairing-code auth
- Released under GPL-3.0
